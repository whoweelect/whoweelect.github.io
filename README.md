# Who We Elect

**Their record. Your judgment.**

Who We Elect is a free, independent, nonpartisan and noncommercial civic information initiative dedicated to making documented government records concerning elected officials more accessible to the public.

## Our Purpose

To support informed civic participation and accountable authority through factual information, transparent research standards and access to original government sources.

Who We Elect does not engage in political campaigning, lobbying, electoral advocacy or candidate endorsements.

## Our Principles

- Evidence before judgment.
- Consistent standards regardless of political affiliation.
- Original, authoritative government sources.
- Clear distinctions between allegations, findings and final dispositions.
- Transparent corrections and editorial accountability.
- Free public access without registration or political advertising.

## Project Status

Who We Elect is currently in public beta. Research and coverage are expanding incrementally.

The absence of a published record does not establish that no relevant record exists.
