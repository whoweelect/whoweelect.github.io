Evidence before judgment

We may delegate authority, but we cannot delegate our responsibility for participation.

Our proposed publication standard requires human verification of official primary sources, including:

  • Department of Justice and official court records
  • House Ethics Committee and Senate Ethics Committee
  • Federal Election Commission enforcement records
  • Congressional Record and official disclosure records

Charges, investigations, findings and convictions are distinct. Records must identify their source, date, status and outcome, including dismissals, acquittals and pardons. We apply the same standards to every member. No anonymous allegations, public submissions, comments or ratings are accepted.

Coverage will be limited to accessible official records. Absence of a published entry is not proof that no record exists. This independent pilot is not affiliated with the U.S. government.
